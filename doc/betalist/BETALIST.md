## Descrizione lunga
Today you pay for one app for every need: a document scanner, a family calendar, a sleep app, a timesheet, an ID-photo app… and you still have no protection against the scams that reach you every day.

KUMANI puts it all in one account:

- **Scam protection, free for everyone** — check a website, an IBAN or a suspicious message before you pay or click. Our community Anti-Scam Network confirms real scams and warns everyone who checks the same site, number or IBAN. In a single day we traced and blocked 19 linked fake crypto casinos.
- **Your life, organised** — MemoLife planner with appointments, tasks and reminders, synced to Google Calendar; expenses and bills; home and car deadlines; documents; travel with shared costs.
- **Tools for people who work with clients** — quotes, clients and jobs, booking page, link in bio, landing page, loyalty cards.
- **No menus to learn** — write your need in your own words (“I got a strange text message”) and KUMI takes you straight to the right tool.
- **Works everywhere** — install it from the browser on Android, iPhone or computer, no app store needed. Available in English and Italian.

Plans: anti-scam tools are free forever; Plus €59/year; Pro €169/year.
